Unicode normalization
Unicode lets the same visible text be encoded in more than one way. The letter é can be a single code point or an e followed by a combining accent, and Arabic-script text may use the Arabic yeh or the Persian yeh for what readers see as the same letter. Before indexing, text is normalized: canonical forms are composed, compatibility characters such as ligatures and full-width letters are folded, case is removed, and optional marks like accents or Arabic diacritics are stripped. The zero-width non-joiner (ZWNJ) is important in Persian because it separates parts of a word without inserting a space.
